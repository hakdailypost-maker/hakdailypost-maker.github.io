What is AI Simply Tool?

AI Simply Tool publishes one short vertical video every day. Each video shows everyday people one useful AI tool, tip or workflow, such as writing an email, planning a week or studying faster, in simple English and in under a minute.

We keep it honest: no hype, no invented statistics, and only real features you can try today.

How the videos are made

The channel is run with a small private app on our own computer, called AI Simply Tool Studio. It writes a short script with the help of an AI model, adds a computer voice and a paper cut-out animation, and uploads the finished video to our own YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram accounts. Scripts and voices are made with AI tools, and we say so in every post.
