Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Who we are

This policy covers the website hakdailypost-maker.github.io and the private app AI Simply Tool Studio ("the app"). The app is used only by the owner of the AI Simply Tool channels to create and publish videos to the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What data the app uses

When the owner connects an account, the app uses that platform's official API with the owner's permission:

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): uploads videos to the owner's channel and reads that channel's video statistics (views, likes, comments, subscribers gained and estimated earnings).
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta Graph API): publishes Reels to the owner's Facebook Page and Instagram account and reads their post statistics.
  • TikTok (Content Posting API): publishes videos to the owner's TikTok account and reads basic account and video statistics.

The app does not collect data about viewers, followers or any other people, and it does not read private messages.

How data is stored and shared

  • Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer and in the owner's private Google Sheet.
  • We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Data is kept only as long as it is useful for the channel's own reporting, and can be deleted at any time.

AI Simply Tool Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google, YouTube and your choices

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Access can be removed at any time from your Google account connections page, in Meta's Business Integrations settings, or in TikTok's "Manage app permissions" settings. Stored data can be deleted on request.

This website

This website has no sign-in, no forms and no tracking or advertising cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts.
